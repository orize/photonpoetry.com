Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 June 2025

This page describes how Luminous handles your information. It is a plain-language summary intended as a placeholder before formal legal review.

What the app does

Luminous lets you build animated lighting scenes from photos. Sprites move across an image and sample nearby colours to drive an in-app lighting preview. Optional integrations with smart-home platforms may be offered in future releases.

Data on your device

Your photos, scenes, and library content are stored locally on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. Luminous does not upload your photo library to our servers for processing or storage.

Optional iCloud sync

When iCloud sync is enabled in a future release, scene and library data may sync through your personal iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit service (iCloud.photonpoetry.com.Luminous). That data remains under your Apple ID and is subject to Apple’s privacy terms. We do not operate separate cloud storage for your scenes today.

Third-party services

If you choose to connect smart-home integrations (such as Home Assistant or Philips Hue), credentials and commands are exchanged directly between the app and those services according to their own policies. Luminous does not sell your personal data.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@photonpoetry.com.
