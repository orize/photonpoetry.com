Frequently asked questions

Which devices are supported?
Luminous is built for iPhone first, with iPad and Mac support planned alongside the iOS release. Check the App Store listing for the current minimum iOS version when it becomes available.
Where are my scenes stored?
Scenes and photo libraries are saved on your device. Optional iCloud sync may be offered in a future update; see Luminous’ privacy policy for details.
How do I report a bug?
Email support@photonpoetry.com with a short description, your device model, and iOS version. Screenshots or screen recordings make the issue easier to reproduce.
